WHO WE ARE

About Thika Eateries

Connecting food lovers with amazing places to eat around Thika, Kenya.

OUR STORY

Discovering the Taste of Thika

Thika is home to a growing food scene filled with restaurants, cafés, food spots and unique culinary experiences.

Thika Eateries was created to make discovering these places easier for residents, visitors and food lovers.

Our platform brings food discovery, restaurant information and local food experiences together in one convenient place.

OUR MISSION

Making Food Discovery Easier

Our mission is to help people discover great places to eat while giving local eateries an opportunity to reach more customers.

OUR VISION

Connecting Thika Through Food

We envision a platform where anyone can easily discover, explore and experience the diverse food culture of Thika.

WHY WE EXIST

More Than Just Food Discovery

Finding a good place to eat should be simple. Thika Eateries brings together useful information about eateries so customers can make better choices.
